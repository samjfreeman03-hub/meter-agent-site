Meter Agent

A private, text-message service that keeps your street parking meter paid until you leave.

Save (833) 549-6051 to your contacts

How it works

  1. Text a photo of your parking meter to (833) 549-6051 and say how long you're staying, for example "2 hours" or "until 4pm".
  2. Meter Agent pays the meter and adds more time before it runs out, using the city's official Text2Pay system.
  3. Ten minutes before your leave time it asks whether you're still parked. Reply DONE when you leave.

How people opt in

Keyword campaign: a user texts PARK (or a photo of their parking meter) to (833) 549-6051. Meter Agent replies with this welcome message:

"Welcome to Meter Agent by FLAIR. I keep your parking meter paid until you leave. Msg frequency varies (about 4-8 msgs per parking session). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Terms: samjfreeman03-hub.github.io/meter-agent-site/terms.html Privacy: samjfreeman03-hub.github.io/meter-agent-site/privacy.html"

Meter Agent never sends the first message. A user opts in by sending the first text message to (833) 549-6051. Every conversation is started by the user. Before a phone number can use the service, the account owner adds it to the approved list; unapproved numbers receive a single reply explaining that the service is private, and nothing further.

Users can stop at any time by replying STOP. Replying HELP returns instructions. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies with how long the user is parked, typically 4 to 8 messages per parking session.
