The fine print

How opting in works, and what you're agreeing to.

Opting in and message details

Keyword campaign: a user texts PARK (or a photo of their parking meter) to (833) 549-6051. Meter Agent replies with this welcome message:

"Welcome to Meter Agent by FLAIR. I keep your parking meter paid until you leave. Msg frequency varies (about 4-8 msgs per parking session). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply HELP for help, STOP to cancel. Terms: samjfreeman03-hub.github.io/meter-agent-site/terms.html Privacy: samjfreeman03-hub.github.io/meter-agent-site/privacy.html"

Meter Agent never sends the first message. A user opts in by sending the first text to (833) 549-6051; every conversation is started by the user. Before a phone number can use the service, the account owner adds it to the approved list; unapproved numbers receive a single reply explaining that the service is private, and nothing further.

Reply STOP at any time to stop. Reply HELP for instructions. Message and data rates may apply. Message frequency varies with how long you're parked, typically 4 to 8 messages per parking session.

What you're agreeing to

Meter Agent pays parking meters on your behalf using the payment method you provide, at the city's posted rate plus the city's per-transaction fee. You are responsible for parking legally, including posted time limits. You'll receive text messages about your parking session only; no marketing.
