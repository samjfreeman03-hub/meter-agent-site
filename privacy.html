Privacy Policy

Meter Agent, operated by FLAIR (a DBA of Sam Freeman Enterprises, LLC). Last updated September 11, 2026.

What we collect

  • Your phone number, used to identify you and send you messages.
  • The messages and photos you send us, used to read the meter number and understand your request. Photos are processed and are not kept after the meter number is read.
  • Your name and email address, if you provide them, used for parking receipts.
  • Payment method details, if you add a card, used only to pay Text-to-Pay meters you ask us to pay. Card details are stored encrypted. Text DELETE CARD at any time to remove them. Any card number sent to us by text is discarded and never stored; the secure link is the only place to enter a card.
  • Parking session records: meter number, times, and amounts paid, kept so you can see what was paid and so we can bill for the service.

How we use it

Only to run the service: paying meters you request, sending you messages about those sessions, and billing. We do not sell or share your information for marketing. Text messaging opt-in data and consent are never shared with third parties for their own purposes.

Who else sees it

  • Twilio, our text-messaging provider, carries the messages.
  • Anthropic, whose AI reads meter photos and interprets your requests.
  • IPS Group, the pay-by-phone processor for Text-to-Pay meters, receives the meter number, duration, payment details, and an email or phone number for receipts.
  • Flowbird is not sent anything by us. At Flowbird meters you pay Flowbird directly from your own phone; we only send you the link and reminders.
  • Railway, our hosting provider, runs the servers that store the data above.

Each of these handles data under its own privacy policy.

Your choices

Reply STOP to stop all messages. Text DELETE CARD to remove your payment method. Email us to have your account and records deleted.
