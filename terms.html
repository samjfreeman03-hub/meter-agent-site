1. The service

Meter Agent is a text-message service that keeps your parking meter paid for the length of time you request and sends you text messages about that parking session. It is available only to phone numbers the operator has approved. How it works depends on the meter:

  • Text-to-Pay meters (IPS Group; most of Los Angeles and West Hollywood): Meter Agent purchases meter time on your behalf through the city's official pay-by-phone system, using the payment method you provide, and re-purchases before each block expires.
  • Flowbird meters (Beverly Hills, Santa Monica): Meter Agent does not purchase time or process payment. It sends you a one-tap link that opens Flowbird's own text-to-pay service on your phone, and reminds you before your time runs low. Payment happens between you and Flowbird.

2. Text messaging consent

By sending a text message to (833) 549-6051 you consent to receive text messages from Meter Agent about your parking session. Messages are transactional and conversational only; we do not send marketing messages. Message frequency varies with how long you are parked. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP at any time to stop receiving messages. Reply HELP for help. Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

3. Your responsibilities

You remain responsible for parking legally. Posted time limits, street-cleaning restrictions, and other rules apply regardless of whether a meter is paid. Meter Agent buys time only when the city's system allows it and will tell you when it cannot. You are responsible for telling Meter Agent when you leave; if you do not reply to a check-in, Meter Agent extends once and then stops paying.

4. Payments

Meter purchases are charged to the payment method you provide, at the city's posted rate plus the city's per-transaction fee. Meter Agent may charge a separate subscription or per-use fee for the service, which will be disclosed to you before it applies. Meter purchases are made with the city's payment processor and are subject to that processor's refund policies.
